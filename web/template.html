    What the laws do not say

    • They cover the decision kernel. Linux, cgroups, seccomp and the controller are tested, not proved.
    • Rust and WebAssembly are matched to the Bend kernel by differential testing.
    • They do not make a classifier accurate. They limit what a wrong one can cause.
    • This page runs no agent and isolates nothing. WSL2 has not been tested yet.
